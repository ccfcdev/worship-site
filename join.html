We audition gently and rehearse seriously. Tell us what you do and a team leader will message you about the next rehearsal.

How it works

1. Apply

Send the form. The team sees it on their dashboard and replies on WhatsApp.

2. Rehearsal

Come and sing or play with the team at a weekly rehearsal.

3. Serve

Join the rota for Sundays, Koinonia and outreach missions.

Who

Members and friends of CCFC who love Jesus and can commit to rehearsals.

play or mix?
